The two-minute award cheat sheet

Last checked 2026-08-08.

Saver space only

United prices awards dynamically on its own flights — the same business seat can be 88,000 miles as a saver award or 360,000 without one. This site lists saver space only: the capacity-controlled awards at prices worth paying. A date missing here may still be bookable on united.com, just at a price you don’t want.

Roundtrips are two one-ways

United prices awards per leg with no roundtrip discount, so every total here is outbound + return, per person. The legs book separately, which also means you can mix cabins — or airlines — between directions.

The −15% card badge

  • Club Infinite + Premier takes 15% off awards on United-operated flights only — never partner metal. That’s why the badge is rare in Business and First: nearly all of that long-haul space is on partners.
  • The card also unlocks extra saver inventory that is only visible logged in — no third-party feed can see it, so united.com may show more than this site does.

Cabin codes

Before you celebrate

  • Saver space appears and vanishes within hours; every listing is a snapshot.
  • seats.aero is a cache, not live inventory, and occasionally shows phantom seats that fail at booking.
  • Always confirm on united.com — the date links do exactly that.
